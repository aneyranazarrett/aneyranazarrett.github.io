Work in Progress

  • Trade Barriers Without Tariffs: Border Tightening and Pass-Through in Retail Markets
    Does U.S. border enforcement reach Mexican consumers? When truck inspections stall freight at Laredo and El Paso, I trace the delay through to prices, stockouts, and product quality on supermarket shelves, comparing suppliers who export through the affected crossings with those who do not.
  • AI Tools for Store Managers: Training, Adaptation, and Store Performance
    Field experiment in preparation with a large retailer in Mexico. One arm trains store managers to use AI in general and observes which tasks they adapt it to; the other trains them on specific tasks that can be done with AI and measures the impact on store performance.
  • Local Security Shocks and the Organization of Work Inside the Firm
    Worker-level records linked to geolocated crime events: who does not come in and at what hour, how shifts are recomposed, and who is moved or promoted afterward.
  • Electoral Business Cycles and Everyday Compliance
    Inventory shrinkage as a measure of everyday compliance, comparing stores in municipalities where the 2019 mayoral race was decided narrowly against stores where it was not.
